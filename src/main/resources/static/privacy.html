Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026 | End Route Logistics

1. Introduction

End Route Logistics ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website end-route-logistics.vercel.app or use our AI-powered carrier estimation engine.

2. Data We Collect

When you utilize our logistics routing platform, we collect operational data necessary to perform route optimization and carrier ranking:

  • Shipment Query Inputs: Origin pincode, destination pincode, cargo type classification, priority preferences, and special handling flags (fragile, perishable).
  • Technical Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referral URLs, and interactions with our search interfaces.

3. Use of External APIs & Third-Party Services

To provide real-time weather alerts and accurate carrier benchmarking, we integrate with trusted third-party service providers:

  • Google Gemini AI & OpenWeather API: Route pincodes are processed to fetch live weather conditions, regional stopover timelines, and cargo risk scores.
  • Google AdSense & Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.

4. Data Retention & Security

We do not store personally identifiable financial records or user passwords. Search inputs are held temporarily in session memory to process your shipment recommendations. We implement industry-standard HTTPS encryption and security protocols to safeguard all transit data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our compliance team at privacy@endroutelogistics.com.
