Transport Law & Pan-India Compliance

Regulatory Framework & Interstate Standards | End Route Logistics

1. Statutory Regulatory Alignment

End Route Logistics structures carrier classification and routing parameters in alignment with Indian transport regulations, including the Carriage by Road Act 2007 and the Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR).

2. Goods & Services Tax (GST) & e-Way Bill Standard

All interstate B2B, cold chain, and bulk freight carriers benchmarked on our platform comply with mandatory e-Way Bill documentation required for commercial consignments exceeding ₹50,000 across Indian states.

3. Specialized Cargo Handling Guidelines

Our scoring algorithm applies automated risk penalties based on regulatory handling norms:

  • Perishable & Cold Chain Freight: Adherence to food safety and temperature control protocols (FSSAI guidelines).
  • Hazardous & Fragile Consignments: Mandatory shock-safe packaging and transport vehicle compliance according to Motor Vehicles Act safety standards.

4. Environmental & Transit Safety

We promote eco-friendly modal shifts (Rail and Multi-modal B2B transit) to reduce carbon emissions across national transport corridors.

5. Compliance Inquiries

For transport compliance inquiries, contact our legal desk at compliance@endroutelogistics.com.
